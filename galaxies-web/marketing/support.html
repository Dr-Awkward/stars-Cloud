keep the lights on

The ads keep the servers on. Donations let me care less about the ads.

Galaxies is free and always will be. If it earns a place in your week, you can chip in. If not, play anyway.

Where the money actually goes

This is the whole list. There is no office, no salary, and no investor waiting to be paid back.

Servers
The machines that hold the galaxy and run turn generation every deadline. Most of the time they idle cheaply; at the deadline they do a hard burst of work for every active game at once, and that burst is the expensive part.
Storage
Game files and every turn in a game's history. Every turn is written once and never overwritten, which is the thing that would let a game be rolled back. Off-project backups are on the list and are not built yet. A game runs for in-game decades across real-life weeks, so the history is not small and it does not get thrown away when the game ends.
The domain and the boring bits
Domain registration, certificates, and the mail that carries your turn notifications so they land in the inbox instead of the spam folder.

Ads pay for most of that. They are standard ads, they run on this marketing site, the game browser and lobby, profile pages, and the game-over summary, and they never appear inside an active game. The map, your orders, and combat are a permanent ad-free zone.

Donations are what let the ad load stay light instead of chasing every last impression. That is the honest trade, and it is the only thing your money buys.

What donating does not buy

Worth being blunt about, because plenty of free games are less blunt.

  • No in-game advantage. Not a resource bonus, not a faster hull, not a better home world, not a hidden setting. A donor and a first-time player sit down to exactly the same rules. A 4X game where money moves the numbers is not worth playing, and it is not what this is.
  • No ad removal for you alone. There is no ad-free tier and there will not be one. Either the ad placement is acceptable for everybody or it gets cut for everybody.
  • No priority support. One person reads the mail. Your message goes in the same queue as everyone else's, and it gets answered when it gets answered.
  • No say you would not otherwise have. Bug reports and design arguments are read on their merit. Sponsoring does not move you up the roadmap.

Two ways in, both of them outbound

Both links leave this site. Galaxies never takes a payment itself, never stores a card, and never asks you for one. There is no subscription to the game, no popup on your third visit, and nothing anywhere behind a paywall.

Sponsor on GitHub Send a tip on Cash App

GitHub Sponsors handles monthly or one-off support, and you can cancel it there whenever you like; nothing here will remind you about it. Cash App is the tip jar for people who do not have a GitHub account and do not want one.

If money is tight, this is the honest alternative

It is a free game. Nobody is under any obligation, and a player who never sends a cent is not a lesser player.

  • File a bug with the turn number and what you expected instead.
  • Finish your turns, so the six people waiting on the deadline are not waiting on you.
  • Tell one person who used to play Stars! that there is a game built on the Stars! Nova engine running again.

Note

Support is invited once, here, and repeated in the footer. It will never be a dialog over your game, an interstitial between turns, or a locked feature.
