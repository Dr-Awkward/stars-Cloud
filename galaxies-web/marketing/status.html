How this page works

This table is written by hand, not wired to a monitor. It is accurate when it is updated and stale the moment something breaks at three in the morning. Trust the incident log below over the table, and trust your own game over both.

There is no SLA

Galaxies is free. A free service run by one person does not get to promise you nines, and any number printed here would be marketing rather than a commitment. So there is no uptime guarantee, no credit if a turn runs late, and no support contract.

What you do get, which is a smaller and more honest list:

  • Turn generation is the thing protected first. If something has to give, it will be the lobby, the profile pages, or this site, not the machinery that resolves your orders.
  • A missed deadline gets extended, not skipped. Half a galaxy resolving is worse than everyone waiting another day, so the turn waits.
  • Incidents that affected play get written down here, including the ones that were my own fault.
  • You are told in plain words what happened, not "a subset of users may have experienced degraded performance".

What counts as an incident

Anything that stopped you playing the game you signed up for: turns that did not generate on time, orders that would not save, sign-in that would not let you in, a map that would not load, turn email that never arrived, or data that came back wrong.

Losing a battle you were sure you would win is not an incident. That is the game working exactly as designed, and I am sorry about your fleet.

Incident log

Newest first. Every entry stays up permanently; nothing is quietly deleted once it stops being embarrassing.

If something is broken and this page says otherwise

Tell me, because the page is hand-written and it may simply be wrong. The useful report includes the game name, the turn number, what you were doing, and what happened instead. Send it to coop@farehard.com.
